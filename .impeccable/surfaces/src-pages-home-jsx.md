# Home (Persuade)

## Scope
- **Surface:** the homepage, `src/pages/Home.jsx`, plus the shared chrome it sets: Navbar and Footer.
- **Visitor mode:** Persuade.
- **Audience:** anyone in Jordan or Lebanon who trains, on phone, tablet or desktop.
- **Job:** choose by goal first, then order the stack on WhatsApp (cash on delivery).
- **Proof on hand:** official distributor, the ISO 9001 / 22000 / 45001 badges, 21 brand logos, real prices, branches.
- **Constraints:**
  - The homepage sections stay the same set, in this order: hero, trust ticker, categories, best sellers, tub teardown, offers, shop by goal, kinetic band, brands marquee, trust numbers and certificates, reels, CTA. They may be restyled.
  - Red and white only, never a full-black theme.
  - No emoji.
  - Arabic must work right-to-left.
- **Memorable moment:** tapping a goal plate loads that goal's products onto the bar.
- **Unresolved:** owner-supplied WhatsApp numbers, Lebanon prices, and the unconfirmed branches.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The homepage is a loaded competition bar, and the visitor's goal is the selection that loads it. This refuses the category default: a hero banner with a tub, a Shop now button and a row of equal product cards.

**OWN-WORLD:**
- **Ground:** chalk-white platform and cool chalk grey.
- **Colour:** competition red `#ED312E` is the only colour, used at plate scale.
- **Rubber black:** only at plate edges and in small text.
- **Steel:** a photographic knurled bar.
- **Type:** condensed stencil plate caps and kg numerals for display, and a sturdy grotesk for body text.
- **Components are plates:**
  - goals, categories and the stack total are calibrated plates;
  - every tub carries a provenance ticket (brand, size, official-stock stamp);
  - the bar is the page's one unbroken line, and a collar marker shows which goal is loaded.

**STORY:** The visitor understands that First Nutrition is the official, certified store for 21 brands, with real local prices. They believe that picking a goal gets them the right stack. They then load a goal and order its stack on WhatsApp, or they browse by category.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:**
- **Header:** the nav runs along the top (red wordmark, links, search, country, العربية, cart).
- **Headline:** "LOAD YOUR GOAL." is centred in stencil caps, with the trust line under it.
- **Bar:** a full-bleed barbell crosses at mid-height, with loaded sleeves at both edges.
- **Goal plates:** four of them sit in a row above the bar, and the selected one is red.
- **Stack:** the selected stack's tubs sit under the bar, each with its name, size and price. The stack total and the red "Order this stack on WhatsApp" button are on the right.
- **Below the fold:** the category plates begin.
